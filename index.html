Rusty

A fast, private web browser for Android with a built-in ad blocker, a built-in WireGuard VPN client and Chrome extension support. Made with Rust.

Download for Android Privacy policy

Free. No account. No ads. No analytics.

Ad and tracker blocking

A built-in ad blocker using community filter lists, plus YouTube ad skipping, cookie banner hiding and tracking-link cleanup.

Built-in VPN

Bring your own WireGuard config and connect from the address bar. Rusty runs the tunnel on your device and never sees your traffic.

Chrome extensions

Install content-script extensions straight from the Chrome Web Store with one tap.

Speed dials and groups

Pin your favourite sites to the start page, organise them into groups, and keep separate dials in a swipe-in sidebar.

Dark pages and themes

Force dark mode on any site, pick your accent colour and wallpaper, and let the toolbars hide as you scroll.

Private by design

Private tabs with an optional screen lock, HTTPS-only mode, pop-up blocking, screenshot blocking and one-tap data clearing.

Handy tools

Paste & go, voice search, save pages offline, reader mode, find in page, auto-scroll, video speed, picture in picture and more.

Your data stays with you

Bookmarks, history and settings live only on your phone. There is nothing to sign in to and no developer server that sees what you browse.

Install

Download the latest version from the Rusty download page and open the file to install it. Android may ask you to allow installs from your browser the first time.

Rusty uses the Android System WebView to display web pages, so keep Android System WebView (or Chrome) up to date from Google Play for the best speed and security.
