Data safety

A plain summary for Rusty. Read the full privacy policy for details. Last updated: 26 November 2026.

QuestionAnswer
Does Cryotap collect your data?Cryotap runs no server and receives nothing from the app. The ad network (Start.io) collects some data to show ads, see below.
Does Cryotap sell or share your data?Cryotap does not sell your data. The ad network receives the data needed to show ads: your advertising ID, IP address, approximate location (and precise location if you have allowed location access), installed apps information and ad interactions. It also collects diagnostics and crash information about its own software, which it does not share. You can reset your advertising ID or turn off location access in Android settings.
Accounts or sign-in?None. There is no account to create or delete.
Ads?Yes. Small banner ads from Start.io appear at the bottom of a few Settings pages (About, Privacy and security, Clean pages and shopping, Focus and speed, Advanced). There are no ads on web pages you visit, none in kid safe mode, and no pop-up, full-screen or video ads. Ads are less personalised unless you turn on Settings → About → Personalised ads.
Analytics or crash reporting?None.
Where are bookmarks, history and settings kept?Only on your device.
Is data encrypted in transit?Sites loaded over HTTPS are encrypted. HTTPS-only mode is available. VPN traffic is encrypted by WireGuard.
Can you delete your data?Yes: Settings → clear data, or uninstall the app.

Services contacted by features you use

As a browser, Rusty sends requests to the websites you visit and, depending on features, to a few services. These are described in the table in section 4 of the privacy policy: your search engine, DuckDuckGo suggestions (optional), the Google favicon service for start page icons (optional), Google Translate for page translation (optional), the SponsorBlock service for skipping YouTube sponsors (optional), VirusTotal file-hash lookups (only when you tap them), ad-blocker filter list hosts, the Chrome Web Store when you install extensions, Google Translate when you choose Translate, and your VPN server when you turn on the VPN.

Permissions

Camera, microphone and location are requested only when a website asks, and only after you allow it. Internet access is required to browse. The VPN service permission is used only for the VPN feature.
