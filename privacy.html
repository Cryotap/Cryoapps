Privacy policy

App: Rusty (Android browser) · Developer: Cryotap · Last updated: 26 November 2026

In short: Rusty has no accounts and no analytics. It shows small banner ads in a few Settings pages, supplied by Start.io. Cryotap does not run any server that receives your browsing data, and does not sell your personal information. Your bookmarks, history and settings are stored only on your device.

1. Who we are

Rusty is a web browser for Android published by Cryotap ("we", "us"). You can contact us at mrdevcat@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information ourselves. Rusty does not have user accounts, does not ask for your name, email address or phone number, and does not include analytics or crash-reporting software. The only advertising software is the Start.io ad SDK described in section 4. We do not receive your browsing history, searches, bookmarks, location, files, contacts or any other data from the app.

3. Information stored on your device

To work as a browser, Rusty stores the following only on your device, inside the app's private storage:

  • Browsing history, open tabs, bookmarks, speed dials and groups.
  • Your settings and choices (for example theme, search engine and privacy options).
  • Cookies, cached files and site data created by the websites you visit.
  • Pages you choose to save for offline reading, and extensions you install along with their stored data.
  • Your VPN configuration, if you import one.
  • Files you download (these are saved to your Downloads folder), and backup files you choose to create.

Private tabs do not save history, and their cookies and site data are removed when you close your last private tab. You can erase everything at any time (see section 9).

4. Connections the app makes

Rusty connects to the internet to load the pages you ask for. Some features also contact third-party services. When they do, that service receives the normal technical information of any internet request, such as your IP address, and the specific data listed here. We do not receive any of it.

5. The built-in VPN

Rusty includes a WireGuard VPN client. We do not provide, operate or recommend any VPN servers. The VPN only works if you import your own WireGuard configuration, and it only starts when you turn it on. It uses Android's VpnService to send your device's internet traffic through the server in your configuration. The tunnel is run locally by the app. We do not see, log, store or share your traffic, your configuration or your IP address. The operator of the VPN server you choose can see your traffic and their own privacy policy applies to it.

6. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load web pages and know when you are offline.
  • Camera, microphone and location: only used when a website you visit asks for them, Rusty asks you first, and Android asks for permission. You can refuse, and change your choice later in Settings.
  • Modify audio settings: so web video and audio can play correctly.
  • Storage (Android 9 and older only): to save files you download.
  • VPN service: only to run the VPN feature described above.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ad network for the banner ads in Settings.
  • Display over other apps, notifications and foreground service: only for the floating mini-browser window you open yourself.
  • Biometrics: only to unlock private tabs, if you turn that on.

Rusty reads the clipboard only when you tap "Paste & go", or, if you turn the option on, to offer to open a copied link.

7. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share personal information, because we do not have any. Data goes to the third parties in section 4 only as a direct result of features you use.

8. Security

Your data is kept in Rusty's private storage, protected by the Android app sandbox. You can lock private tabs behind your screen lock, enable HTTPS-only mode, block pop-ups and trackers, and block screenshots. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, so please also keep your device and Android System WebView up to date.

9. Retention and deleting your data

Because everything is stored on your device, you are in control of it:

  • In the app, open Settings and use the clear data options for history, cookies, cache and site data. You can also set Rusty to clear your data when you close it.
  • Uninstalling Rusty removes all of its stored data from your device.
  • Files you downloaded and backup files you created are saved in your Downloads folder or the place you chose. Delete them with any file manager.

Rusty has no accounts, so there is no account to delete, and we hold no data about you on our side to remove.

10. Children

Rusty is a general-purpose browser and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. Ads are not shown while kid safe mode is on.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the UK GDPR, EU GDPR or California privacy law), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. We do not hold personal data about you. If you have a question, contact us at the address above.

12. Third-party websites

Websites you open and extensions you install are not controlled by us and have their own privacy practices. Please review them.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change how Rusty handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.

14. Contact

Questions or requests: mrdevcat@gmail.com.
